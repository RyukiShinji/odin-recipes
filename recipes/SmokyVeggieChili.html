Smoky Veggie Chilli

A smoky veggie chili is a plant-based, hearty stew that mimics the deep, complex flavors of traditional meat-based chili by using smoky spices, fire-roasted vegetables, and umami-rich ingredients. It typically features a base of diverse beans, peppers, and tomatoes, enhanced by smoky elements like chipotle peppers or smoked paprika to create a rich, warming, and comforting dish.

ingredients :

  • Smoke and Heat: Smoked paprika (1–2 tsp), chipotle peppers in adobo sauce (1-3 peppers, chopped), or a splash of liquid smoke.
  • Beans: 2–3 cans of beans (black, kidney, pinto, or chickpeas), rinsed and drained
  • Vegetables & Aromatics: Onion (red or yellow), garlic (3–4 cloves), bell peppers (red or green), and celery.
  • Tomatoes: 28-ounce can of crushed or diced fire-roasted tomatoes.
  • Base: Vegetable broth or water.
  • Spices: Chili powder, ground cumin, dried oregano, and optionally, cocoa powder or tamari for depth.

steps :

  1. Sauté Aromatics: Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add diced onions, peppers, and celery, cooking until softened (about 8–10 minutes).
  2. Bloom Spices: Add minced garlic, smoked paprika, cumin, and chili powder. Stir for 1 minute until fragrant to "bloom" the spices.
  3. Simmer: Add canned tomatoes, beans, vegetable broth, and chopped chipotle peppers. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer uncovered for 20–30 minutes to allow flavors to meld and liquid to thicken.
  4. Finish: Stir in a tablespoon of lime juice or apple cider vinegar to brighten the flavors just before serving.
